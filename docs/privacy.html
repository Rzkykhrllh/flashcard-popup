Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Kotoba Bank is a Chrome extension for learning Japanese vocabulary. It is built to work entirely inside your browser. This page explains what it stores and what it does with page content.

What we collect

Nothing. Kotoba Bank has no server, no account system and no analytics. The developer does not receive any of your data.

What is stored on your device

  • Your words, collections, notes and review progress — in the browser's IndexedDB.
  • Your settings (theme, card position, reader preferences, the current Study session) — in chrome.storage.local.

This data stays on your computer. It is removed if you uninstall the extension or clear site data. You can export it at any time from the Words tab (Backup → Export JSON).

Page content

To show flashcards and highlight words you've saved, the extension runs on the pages you visit (the "Read and change all your data on all websites" permission). It reads the visible text of the page locally to find Japanese words that are in your bank. Page text is never stored, logged or sent anywhere, and the page itself is not modified — highlights are drawn with the browser's CSS Highlight API.

Network requests

  • The Kotoba Bank page loads a font (Zen Maru Gothic) from Google Fonts.
  • Optional Google Sheets sync (currently hidden) fetches only the sheet URL you enter.
  • Future versions may download a dictionary file once from this project's GitHub Releases. No personal data is sent with that request.

Permissions

  • storage — save your settings.
  • alarms — schedule pop-up flashcards.
  • scripting and access to all sites — show flashcards and highlights on the pages you visit.
